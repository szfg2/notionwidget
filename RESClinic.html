GINA assessment of asthma control

Adults, adolescents and children 6–11 y · GINA 2020

In the past 4 weeks, has the patient had…

Daytime symptoms more than twice/week?
Reliever needed more than twice/week?
Any night waking due to asthma?
Any activity limitation due to asthma?

Levels

Well controlledNone of these
Partly controlled1–2 of these
Uncontrolled3–4 of these
  • Reliever count excludes doses taken before exercise. On MART, count the as-needed ICS-formoterol doses.
  • Symptoms: shortness of breath, cough, chest tightness, wheeze.
  • This grades symptom control only — assess exacerbation risk, fixed airflow limitation, inhaler technique, adherence and side effects separately.
Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA), 2020.

  • Nodules <6 mm may still warrant a 12-month scan if the morphology is suspicious, upper lobe, or high-risk patient.
  • Multiple nodules: use the most suspicious nodule as the guide to management.

Pure ground-glass nodule

Small (<6 mm) and multiple Consider viral infection or other conditions (e.g. RB-ILD). If considered non-inflammatory: follow up 3 mo, 6 mo, then annually ×3 y.
<6 mm, indeterminatevisible sub-solid component, or significant risk factors Follow up 3 mo, 6 mo, then annually ×4 y. PET / guided biopsy not recommended.
Significant size (≥6 mm) LDCT 6, 18, 30, 42, 60 mo. PET and CT-guided biopsy not recommended.

Part-solid nodule

Any size, small solid componentsolid component <6 mm LDCT 3, 9, 18, 30, 42, 60 mo. Consider taking key images for future comparison.
Any size, big solid componentsolid component ≥6 mm Histological correlation suggested. If biopsy not appropriate, PET is an option where the solid component is >1 cm. Where appropriate, consider MDT or chest radiologist discussion.
NB: this guidance applies only to patients aged >35 without a history of primary cancer or immunocompromise. Peri-fissural nodules and lung cancer screening cases are not covered.
