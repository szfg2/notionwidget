How to read this

  • Pleural work dominates. Nearly a third of everything is a pleural effusion question — and if you add the malignant and parapneumonic effusions, pleural disease is 34/108. Most of these are requests for a diagnostic or therapeutic tap, drain, or thoracoscopy decision.
  • Malignancy cuts across categories. Counting suspected lung cancer/masses (10) plus malignant pleural effusions (7) gives ~17 letters (16%) where cancer is the central question — a malignant effusion is filed under Pleural here but is equally a cancer referral.
  • Infection (pneumonia/abscess 6 + parapneumonic/empyema 5 + TB 5) accounts for another 16 letters (~15%).
  • Classification rule: each letter is tagged by the primary reason the team referred, taken from the opening line of your generated reply. Effusions in a patient on active TB treatment are filed under Tuberculosis; a COPD exacerbation in a patient with background cancer is filed under COPD.
  • Source: resblue-patients.json + resblue-archive/ (private szfg2/patient-data). No names or identifiers are used anywhere above.
