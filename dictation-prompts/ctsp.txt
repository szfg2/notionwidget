TEMPLATE: CTSP Note (Called To See Patient)

A short ward note documenting a call to review a patient. Use these exact
headers, in this order; omit any section the doctor gave no information for.
Leave one blank line between sections.

Title line: "CTSP <presenting complaint>" — the complaint is the reason the
doctor was called, taken from the transcript, sentence case (e.g. "CTSP Chest
pain", "CTSP Desaturation", "CTSP Fall"). If no complaint is clearly stated,
write "CTSP __".

"Subjective" — the history as given: what the problem is, when it started, its
character, and any associated symptoms or explicit negatives the doctor stated.
One detail per plain line, in the order spoken.

"O/E" — examination findings, one per plain line. If vitals were dictated, they
go on ONE line at the top of this section, comma-separated in the doctor's own
order (e.g. "BP 138/82, HR 96, RR 18, SpO2 97% RA, T 37.1"). Then the rest of
the examination.

"Impression: <text>" — on a single line, the doctor's stated impression only.
Keep "TRO" and other abbreviations as spoken.

"Plan" — management items, one per plain line, in the order spoken. If the
doctor dictated escalation parameters, they go on the last line of this section
(e.g. "Inform doctor if HR > 110, BP systolic less than 90, or worsening chest
pain"). If no escalation parameters were dictated, do NOT add a line — never
invent thresholds.

FORMATTING FOR THIS TEMPLATE
- No bullet markers anywhere. Every line, in every section, starts at the left
  margin as plain text.
- Telegraphic ward shorthand; keep the doctor's abbreviations (SOB, HS1 + S2,
  A SNT, TRO, PRN).
- Never invent findings, negatives, vitals, thresholds or plans that were not
  dictated.

EXAMPLE
Transcript: "CTSP chest pain, patient says chest pain started about an hour ago,
it's central, no shortness of breath, no radiation, poke in sensation, no
giddiness, on exam heart sounds one and two present lungs are clear abdomen soft
non tender calves supple, impression chest pain to rule out acute myocardial
injury, plan ECG stat, three sets of troponin, chest x-ray, and tell me if the
heart rate goes above one ten or systolic below ninety or the chest pain gets
worse"

Output:
CTSP Chest pain

Subjective
Patient reports chest pain starting an hour ago
Central in nature
No SOB
No radiation
Poke in sensation
No giddiness

O/E
HS1 + S2
Lungs clear
A SNT
Calves supple

Impression: chest pain TRO acute myocardial injury

Plan
ECG stat
Trop 3 sets
CXR
Inform doctor if HR > 110, BP systolic less than 90, or worsening chest pain
