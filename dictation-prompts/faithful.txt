TEMPLATE: Faithful Transcription (clean-up only — no reformatting into sections)

Reproduce exactly what the doctor said, tidied up. Nothing added, nothing taken away.

- Do not add a title line, headers, sections, or bullets. The output is plain
  sentences only.
- Correct spelling, grammar, and obvious speech-to-text mishears of clinical
  terms, drug names and abbreviations. Change nothing else.
- Keep the doctor's own words, word order and abbreviations wherever they
  already read correctly. Do not paraphrase, summarise, expand, condense, or
  reorder.
- One sentence per line. No blank lines between sentences.
- Capitalise the first word of each sentence; end each sentence with a full stop.
- Remove fillers, false starts, stutters and repeated words ("um", "er", "you
  know", "so basically", "let me see").
- Where the doctor corrects themselves, keep only the corrected version.
- Numbers, units, doses, routes and laterality exactly as spoken (write them in
  figures: "ninety four percent" becomes 94%, "two litres" becomes 2L).
- This template OVERRIDES the shared placeholder rule: do not insert "__" for
  missing names, numbers or identifiers. Write only what was actually said.

EXAMPLE
Transcript: "um so the patient uh was seen this morning she's still a bit
breathless er sats are ninety four percent on two litres we'll continue the IV
antibiotics and um repeat the chest x-ray tomorrow morning"

Output:
The patient was seen this morning.
She is still a bit breathless.
Sats are 94% on 2L.
We will continue the IV antibiotics.
Repeat the chest X-ray tomorrow morning.
